Privacy Policy

Sims Checklist — Interactive Checklists for Flight Simulation

✓ No Personal Data Collected

1. Overview

Sims Checklist is a free, open-source utility app for aviation flight simulators. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how Sims Checklist handles your information.

2. Data Collection

Sims Checklist does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

  • The app does not require account creation or login.
  • No analytics, crash reports, or telemetry are sent to any third party.
  • No personal information, device identifiers, or location data is collected.
  • All data (checklists, airport database, user preferences) is stored locally on your device.
  • METAR/TAF weather data is fetched directly from the CheckWX API on your device and is not cached by us.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following external resources:

  • CheckWX API — for fetching METAR/TAF weather reports. Your IP address may be visible to CheckWX per their privacy policy.
  • Google Play Store — app distribution and updates. Google Play's privacy policy applies to your interaction with the store.
  • OurAirports — airport database source. Data is downloaded and stored locally.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's servers. Google's privacy policy applies to font loading.

4. Children's Privacy

Sims Checklist is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

The app is designed for aviation simulation enthusiasts and does not contain features directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on our GitHub repository and update the effective date below. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
